The Self-Journal of Science (SJS) is a non-commercial, multidisciplinary repository that provides journal-like services to entrust the evaluation, classification and communication of research to the unrestricted collective intelligence of the scientific community itself. SJS is a free and open agora where scholars are able to discuss, debate, classify, and evaluate their works in an open and transparent manner that rewards collaboration. In SJS, every article is the beginning of a global scientific conversation.

We believe that only an open collaboration motivated by a genuine interest to protect Science from the interests of commercial enterprises and the self-righteousness of closed academic groups can bring real change.
