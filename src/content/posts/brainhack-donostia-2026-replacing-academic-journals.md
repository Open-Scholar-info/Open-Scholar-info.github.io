On 4 November Pandelis Perakakis gave a keynote at BrainHack Donostia 2026, organised by the Basque Center on Cognition, Brain and Language (BCBL) in San Sebastián. The talk, *Replacing academic journals with repositories and peer communities*, argued that journals bundle three processes, validation, evaluation and dissemination, and that repositories and communities of peers can carry out each of them without journals. It closed with the example of *Psicológica*, the journal of its academic society, which publishes on DIGITAL.CSIC, the institutional repository of the Spanish National Research Council.

At the hackathon Pandelis also pitched reprocheck, an automated reproducibility check that reruns the authors' deposited code on their data and reports what each script produces, so that a checker can compare it with the article.

The slides and a full account can be found [here](https://pandelisperakakis.info/blog/brainhack-donostia-2026.html).
