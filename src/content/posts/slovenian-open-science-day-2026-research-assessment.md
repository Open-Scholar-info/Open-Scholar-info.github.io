On 19 November Pandelis Perakakis gave a keynote at the Slovenian Open Science Day 2026 in Ljubljana, the EOSC National Tripartite Event, before an audience of policy makers, heads of evaluation agencies and university rectors. Drawing on the experience of Complutense University of Madrid, he argued that implementing CoARA takes leaders willing to bear the cost of change, a gradual path from low to high stakes, and detailed tools for evaluators. At UCM that path has gone from the doctoral award to a permanent researcher position and now to the evaluation of 22 research institutes.

His closing argument was that the debate on publishing models distracts from the real issue, which is evaluation. Once evaluation looks at content and not at where research is published, all that publication needs is what already exists, institutional repositories and close collaboration between librarians and researchers.
