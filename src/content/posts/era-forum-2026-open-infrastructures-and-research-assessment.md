On 19 June 2026 Pandelis Perakakis gave the opening talk at the European Commission's ERA Forum session *Synergies between Research Assessment Reform and Open Infrastructures*, invited to offer a researcher's perspective. He argued that Europe has already funded every piece of an open, repository-based system for scholarly communication and evaluation (institutional repositories, Zenodo, OpenAIRE, COAR Notify and the CoARA reform process), but has funded them as separate projects that researchers cannot use as one system. Two policy actions would connect them. Only research deposited in open repositories should be evaluated, as ANECA has begun to require in Spain, and the peer reviews produced in publicly funded evaluations should be deposited as open, citable objects.

The interactive diagram of the talk is on [Pandelis's website](https://pandelisperakakis.info/blog/era-forum-talk-2026.html), together with a longer essay, [The infrastructure already exists. Europe never connected it](https://pandelisperakakis.info/blog/era-forum-open-infrastructures.html).
