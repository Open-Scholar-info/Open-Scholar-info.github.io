### What is Open Scholar?

Open Scholar C.I.C. is a **not-for-profit organisation** whose activities, assets and profits are dedicated to the purpose of providing benefit to the **scientific community**. Our **mision** is to develop ideas and tools that promote **open** and **transparent** **scientific collaboration** for a more **fast**, **efficient** and **natural** **organisation**, **evaluation** and **dissemination** of **global knowledge**.

---

### Activities

Our flagship project is the development and promotion of LIBRE (LIBerating REsearch). LIBRE is a multidisciplinary research platform implementing an innovative evaluation model that we call [author-guided open peer review](http://www.openscholar.info/open-peer-review/ "Open Peer Review").

At the same time, we are interested in organising or participating in local and international **events promoting scientific collaboration** and tools for **alternative evaluation** and **dissemination** of **scientific results**, especially in **developing countries** with limited financial resources.

---

### Organisation and Governance

Open Scholar C.I.C. is a **not-for-profit** [Community Interest Company](http://www.bis.gov.uk/cicregulator "C.I.C.") founded in 2012 and incorporated in England & Wales as a **company limited by guarantee** with company number 08129292. The registered office address is:

The formal governance of Open Scholar C.I.C. is provided by company directors in close collaboration with the working team, members and the advisory board. All roles are voluntary. Any scholar interested in [joining our efforts](http://www.openscholar.info/how-to-join/ "How To Join") to promote openness and transparency in scientific collaboration can join as a company member. Open Scholar members are expected to meet a [minimum list of requirements](http://www.openscholar.info/os_members/ "Members") and have the right to propose new ideas in general meetings and vote on all important decisions regarding the organisation's policies and future directions.
